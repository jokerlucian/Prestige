About Us

Nestled in the heart of Oakham, our studio was born from a passion for transforming skin into living art. What started as a dream in 2015 has blossomed into Rutland's most trusted tattoo destination.

We don't just tattoo. We listen. We sketch. We obsess over every line, every shade, every curve until the vision in your head becomes something permanent and breathtaking on your skin.

Our studio is a sanctuary for those who believe the body is a canvas and life is too short for ordinary art. From bold traditional work to delicate fine-line pieces — our artists bring mastery and meaning to every session.

Oakham is a small town, but the art we create here travels the world on the skin of people who dared to make a statement.

This is Prestige. This is permanent. This is yours.
